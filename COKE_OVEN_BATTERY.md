# Coke Oven Battery — vertical slice

Canonical size: **11 × 6 × 17** (`X=0..10`, `Y=0..5`, `Z=0..16`). The controller faces outward from the front (`Z=0`); positive local Z runs toward the rear. `CokeBatteryMultiblock.expectedAt` is the only source of pattern data for validation and the future CRE-55 guide.

## Exact layout

- `Y=0`: 11×17 reinforced-concrete foundation.
- `Y=1..3`, `Z=2..13`: chambers at `X=4..6`; refractory at `X=3,7`; required air at `X=1,2,8,9`; Mine Frame shell at `X=0,10`.
- `Y=1..3`, `Z=0..1,14..16`: solid service heads. Their outside skin (`X=0,10` or `Z=0,16`) is Mine Frame and their core is refractory, except the four fixed interfaces below.
- `Y=4`: full roof; Mine Frame ribs span X at `Z=2,5,8,11`, all other cells are refractory.
- `Y=5`: Mine Frame perimeter; all 9×15 interior cells are required air.
- Controller: `(5,2,0)`.
- Input Port: `(3,1,0)`.
- Output Port: `(7,1,0)`.
- reused Mine Power Port: `(5,1,16)`.

## Processing and power

- Reaction remains exactly `1 Coal -> 1 Industrial Coke`.
- Input and output ports each expose four stacks (256 items maximum).
- External automation may only insert coal into the input and only extract coke from the output.
- A cycle handles up to 8 items, including safe partial batches.
- Provisional balance: 200 ticks (10 seconds) per batch. This is intentionally a named constant pending a real heated-Mixer benchmark.
- Working demand is 25 kW through the existing EE Mine Power Port device. The first slice accepts 500–600 V and at least 21.25 kW; standby demand remains 120 W.
- The full pattern is scanned once per second while processing and once per five seconds while idle. Right-click forces immediate validation.

## Manual test checklist

### In-game build guide

- Empty-hand right-click the Coke Battery Controller to toggle the guide.
- `Page Up`: next layer (`Y=0..5`).
- `Page Down`: previous layer.
- `Home`: toggle between the selected layer and all layers.
- Blue outline: missing ordinary block; cyan: missing functional Controller/Input/Output/Power position; red: wrong existing block; orange: occupied required-air cell.
- Correct cells are hidden. The action bar identifies the nearest mismatch, its local/world coordinates and the remaining count for that expected type.

1. Build the north-facing structure and verify `843/843` on controller interaction.
2. Repeat for east, south and west controller facings.
3. Replace a required block and occupy one required-air cell; both must invalidate the structure.
4. Connect the rear power port to EE at the nominal voltage and confirm a roughly 25 kW load while running.
5. Test disconnected, undervoltage and insufficient-power states; progress must pause without consuming coal.
6. Insert coal manually and with Create funnels/chutes/belts; reject all non-coal input.
7. Extract Industrial Coke manually through automation; no insertion into the output is allowed.
8. Fill all four output slots; the controller must report Output Blocked without consuming input.
9. Break a structural block during a cycle; processing must stop on the next active validation (within one second).
10. Unload/reload the chunks mid-cycle and confirm inventories and persisted progress are retained.
11. Save/restart a dedicated server and repeat inventory and state checks.
12. Break either inventory port and confirm its contents drop exactly once.
